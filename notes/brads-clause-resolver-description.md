# Clause Resolver

Clause Resolver acts as an expert contract negotiation mediator, helping you transform competing clause proposals into a single aligned clause that's more acceptable to both parties. The agent analyzes your clause and the counterparty's clause, identifies underlying interests, and drafts an optimized solution with complete negotiation guidance.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Provide Your Inputs

You'll fill out a form with:

- **Baseline Agreement**: Upload your contract file (PDF/Word) or paste the text. Full agreement preferred to catch dependent provisions.
- **Clause Location**: Specify the section number/heading or describe the topic (e.g., "liability cap", "SLA credits").
- **Clause A (Your Clause)**: Your party's proposed clause text.
- **Clause B (Counterparty Clause)**: The counterparty's proposed clause text.
- **Your Role**: Which party you represent (Customer, Vendor, Buyer, Supplier, or Other).
- **Drafting Approach**: Choose whether to base the aligned clause on Clause A, Clause B, or draft from scratch.
- **Optional Context**: Playbook requirements, counterparty's rationale, business context.

### Step 2: Review Initial Analysis

The agent generates a comprehensive analysis with 9 components:

1. **Aligned Clause** - Copy-ready clause text
2. **Change Summary** - What changed and why (up to 10 bullets)
3. **Delta Resolution Map** - How each clause version treats the issue and what interests are protected
4. **Dependent Edits** - Required changes to related provisions
5. **Friction Forecast** - Friction score (0-10) and likely objections with response scripts
6. **Internal Negotiation Notes** - Priority stack, fallbacks, trade logic, risk flags
7. **External Negotiation Notes** - Diplomatic explanation for the counterparty
8. **Implementation Notes** - Operational compliance requirements
9. **Open Issues** - Missing information that affects the analysis

### Step 3: Request Revisions

Provide feedback to refine the analysis:

- **Revision Posture**: Choose whether to keep as-is, be more protective, more balanced, or more concessionary.
- **Must-Change Items**: Specific changes you need.
- **Red Lines**: Non-negotiables.
- **Counterparty Won't Accept**: Known deal-breakers.

The agent generates a complete revised analysis with all 9 components updated.

### Step 4: Scenario Testing

Test the clauses with a real-world scenario:

- Provide your own scenario, or
- Let the agent generate a high-risk scenario based on the clause topic

You'll get a comparative analysis showing how Clause A, Clause B, and the aligned clause perform, including practical steps, costs, timelines, and dispute risks.

### Step 5: Export to Word

Download a professional Word document containing:
- Initial analysis (all 9 components)
- Revised analysis (all 9 components)
- Scenario-based risk analysis

## Tips for Best Results

- **Provide full agreement**: More context helps identify dependent provisions.
- **Be specific about context**: Playbook requirements and counterparty rationale improve accuracy.
- **Use the revision step**: The first version is a starting point—refine it based on your needs.
- **Test realistic scenarios**: Choose scenarios that expose real operational challenges.

## What This Agent Does NOT Do

- Provide legal advice or enforceability opinions
- Make litigation predictions
- Reconcile unrelated differences across the entire agreement
- Replace attorney review (use this as a drafting tool, not legal counsel)
